## What

Low findings of the m4-25 review. Triage decides which are worth fixing.

1. **A straggling `request_resource` can take a lock for an ended attempt.** `AttemptTool.liveAttempt` is a separate read from the lock change in `ResourceTools.withLocks` (`ResourceTools.kt`, around lines 82 to 90), and the free (`Orchestrator.kt`, around line 1455) runs before `attempt_finished` is recorded. Unreachable in normal operation, since the process group is killed and empty before the free runs; it needs a survivor of the group SIGKILL, and the next startup frees it. Hardening: `ResourceGrants` keeps the ids passed to `endAttempt`, and each tick frees any own row of an ended attempt (also bl-204 item 6).
2. **No core timeout on provider `prepare` and `release`, and the mutex is held across them** (`ResourceGrants.kt`, around lines 54 to 78 and 135 to 142). A hung provider stalls every grant and every attempt end of the run; a healthy minute long `prepare` also serializes everything behind it. m4-27 and m4-28 require timeouts on their own calls; consider a core side timeout or provider calls outside the mutex. Input for m4-27.
3. **The tick is gated on the current workflow's `resources:`** (`RunCommand.kt`, around line 500). After a reload that empties it, an in-flight attempt polls `preparing` forever, marked releases never run, and locks granted to this project are never prepared. Keep ticking once resources were declared in the run.
4. **A prepare outcome can be lost**, leaving a prepared resource with no provider `release`: on shutdown the tick is canceled while `prepare` blocks and the result is never written (`endAttempt` then skips `release`, `ResourceGrants.kt` around lines 112 to 115), and a failed `locks.prepared` write (around line 75) makes the next tick prepare again with no release. The next startup's `recover` cleans it. m4-27 and m4-28 need an idempotent `prepare`.
5. **A plugin `Error` kills the run.** `guarded` catches `Exception` only (`ResourceGrants.kt`, around lines 135 to 142); a `LinkageError` from a provider jar fails the tick's `launch` and cancels the scope around the orchestrator (`RunCommand.kt`, around lines 490 to 513), and from `endAttempt` it skips `dropRequests` and `deleteDirectory`. Catch `Throwable` except `CancellationException`, and give the free its own try (bl-204 item 9).
6. **A lock freed as abandoned gets no provider `release`.** Documented (`docs/spec-decisions.md`, around line 2935); the next holder's `prepare` runs against what the dead run left.
7. **The schema 8 to 9 downgrade is not documented.** An older binary refuses a version 9 store until `PRAGMA user_version = 8`. "No schema change" (`docs/spec-decisions.md`, around line 2935) sits five lines below the version 9 paragraph and reads as a contradiction; Loop's own reviewer note on the issue said "no schema change" because its diff was cut. See bl-204 item 10 and bl-191.
8. **`ResourceLock.prepared` changed from `Map` to `Map?` with three new fields** in a public plugin-api data class, undocumented (`plugin-api` `ResourceLocks.kt`, around lines 67 to 70).
9. **A tick that keeps failing logs WARN every 500 ms** and fails every tool call with `store` (`RunCommand.kt`, around lines 500 to 505).
10. **`projectRunLive` runs inside the write transaction** on every tick and request whenever another project has rows, and the tick takes `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` twice a second then.
11. **Test gaps:** a hung provider, a straggler, empty resources after a reload, a canceled prepare, malformed snapshot `resources`, a throwing `live`, a failed provider load, a failed `freeAttempt`.
12. **`ResourceProvider.prepare(ResourceDeclaration)` gets no attempt id**, but m4-27 wants the simulator named with the attempt id. Input for m4-27.
