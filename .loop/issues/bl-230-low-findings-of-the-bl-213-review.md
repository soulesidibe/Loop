6. **A resumed session claimed but never run or stopped keeps its folder live until the JVM dies.** One doc line.
7. **Observation:** a turn killed by a guard or the protected roots check now lets the next turn of the same session respawn and load; the checks all rerun, no bypass found. Confirm the orchestrator stops the session after a guard's `TurnFailed`.
8. **Operator note:** a revert leaves `$LOOP_HOME/agy` folders the old sweep never removes.
