## Parent

[Serialized delivery](../specs/serialized-delivery.md). Backlog, not scheduled in a milestone.

## What

Under bl-172's delivery queue only the oldest merge wait of a project advances. A queued delivery that conflicts with the current base waits its turn, then spends a whole merge round only to find the conflict and go back to its agent. `git merge-tree --write-tree <base> <head>` (git 2.38 or later) tells whether a merge conflicts, with no workspace write, no slot and no lock.

Desired behavior:

- Each time Loop fetches the base of a project (after a merge lands, or at the base fetch of a tick), it runs `git merge-tree --write-tree` of the fetched base tip and the verified head, for every merge wait of that project that is queued behind the head of the queue. The head of the queue itself is left to the normal merge check.
- It runs through the issue's own workspace and `WorkspaceGit`, under the same isolation as the other git calls of the merge check, read only. It never writes the worktree, the index or a ref.
- A conflicting queued wait is refused at once, the same way as a conflict found by the merge check today: the m2-26 conflict comment naming the conflicting paths, the wait ends, and the issue goes back to its agent for a new attempt. It leaves the queue; its next delivery queues anew behind the others, like any new delivery.
- A clean result changes nothing. A failure of the `merge-tree` call itself (non zero exit other than conflict, git too old, missing objects) logs a WARN and changes nothing: the normal merge check still finds the conflict later.
- The check runs at most once per queued wait per base tip. A wait whose head and base tip are both unchanged since its last check is not checked again.
- Event `merge_conflict_early` (`issue`, `base`, `paths`) is recorded, and shown by `loop trace` and the human log.
- Document it in the m3-15 section of `docs/spec-decisions.md` and in `.loop/specs/serialized-delivery.md`.

Area: the merge check (`Merges.kt`), the delivery queue of bl-172, `WorkspaceGit`. In `Orchestrator.kt` grep, never read it whole.

Out of scope: rebasing or resolving the conflict, checking the head of the queue early, any change to the merge check itself, other trackers' conflict detection.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `./gradlew --no-daemon :spec-core:test --tests 'loop.core.orchestrator.MergeCheckOrchestratorTest'` passes, with tests for: a queued wait that conflicts with the base is sent back to its agent with the conflict comment before its turn; a queued wait that merges cleanly stays queued; the head of the queue is not checked early; a failing `merge-tree` call leaves the wait queued; an unchanged base and head are not checked twice.
- [ ] `./gradlew --no-daemon build` passes.
