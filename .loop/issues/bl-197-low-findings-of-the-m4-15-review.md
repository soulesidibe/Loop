## What

Low findings of the m4-15 review. Triage decides which are worth fixing.

1. **A retry can be judged on a copy up to one poll old.** In `RetryCoordinator.kt` (`recentlyRead`, `fireDue`), a retry refreshed less than one poll ago is judged on `lastRead` when the polled candidates lack it. If the issue went terminal or inactive within the interval, it is judged eligible on the stale copy; it cannot dispatch (due retries are filtered to the polled eligible ids), but it is neither dropped nor requeued until the cache expires, with no signal. At most one poll of delay.
2. **The stored `dueAt` lags the in memory one.** `RetryCoordinator.requeueNoSlot` moves `dueAt` in memory only. Documented and harmless, since the retry is due from `firstDueAt`; after a restart `waitingFor` is null, so one extra `retry_scheduled` is recorded.
3. **`refreshedAt` and `waitingFor` reset on any new schedule.** The `RetryEntry` is rebuilt in `schedule`. Read and tested; listed for triage to confirm the intent.
4. **`loop trace` shows less on same reason requeues.** Requeues for the same reason are now silent (one event per reason change), and `OrchestratorNoProgressTest` was changed from two requeue events to one on purpose. Intended, worth a line in the docs if missing.

Area: `RetryCoordinator.kt`, `RetryQueue.kt`, `TrackerSlots.kt`, `SlotPlan.kt`.
