## What

Low findings of the m4-42 reviews. Triage decides which are worth fixing.

1. **The 2.5 s close shares the 10 s grace.** It comes out of the same window that covers attempt unwinding and the store close, so an unwind slower than about 7.5 s with a hanging collector still exits 143. Untested.
2. **A slow but healthy collector loses data at stop.** A collector slower than 1 s, or with a backlog, loses open spans and queued events. Reported and documented.
3. **The dropped count is approximate.** `pending` is read while a lane may still be mid record (`ExporterPlugins.kt`, around line 289).
4. **The closer thread catches only `Exception`.** An `Error` from a plugin's close goes to the uncaught handler (`ExporterPlugins.kt`, around lines 277 to 281).
5. **"The default shutdown budget fits" asserts constants only** (`ExportingSinkTest`).
6. **Spans are unbounded.** A span holds every event of its turn or attempt in memory until it ends (`OtelEventExporter.kt`, around lines 103 and 273), so a long turn becomes one large span a collector's request size limit may refuse, losing the whole span.
7. **The `event=plugins outcome=found` line is unconditional** in `RunCommand.exportingSink`, also for projects with no `exporters:`; `attempt_started` rows also gain setup keys (additive).
8. **The JUL logger `io.opentelemetry` is turned off for the whole JVM** when the exporter is enabled (`OtelExporterProvider.kt`, around line 38).
9. **Only the last 256 attempts keep open spans** (`OtelEventExporter.kt`, around lines 43 and 299). Documented.
10. **A `create` that throws ends the run as an internal error.** `exportingSink` runs after the store opens and the run lock is taken (`RunCommand.kt`, around line 268), so it is not reported as a preflight problem.
11. **Queue full drops and export failures share one count** in the `event=export outcome=failed` line, and the SDK's own log is off.

Area: `cli/.../ExporterPlugins.kt`, `cli/.../RunCommand.kt`, `plugins/export-otel` (`OtelEventExporter.kt`, `OtelExporterProvider.kt`).
