## What

Low findings of the m4-27 review. Triage decides which are worth fixing.

1. **Idempotent prepare is only covered by a seeded state test.** Add a test that calls `prepare` twice with the same resource and pid and asserts one simulator remains, and one for a `prepare` that failed after `create`.
2. **`loop run` startup runs `xcrun simctl list devices -j` on every Mac,** even with no resources declared (`RunCommand.kt`, around line 276, `AbandonedAttempts.kt`, around lines 139 to 143). `Xcrun.available()` only checks that `xcrun` is on the PATH, and `/usr/bin/xcrun` always exists, so a Mac without Xcode logs a WARN `recover failed ... exited 72` on every run, and a hung `CoreSimulatorService` delays startup up to 60 s. Skip `recover` when `xcrun --find simctl` fails, or for a kind no workflow declares.
3. **`forEach(::remove)` aborts at the first failing simulator** in `recover` and `prepare` (`IosSimulatorProvider.kt`, around lines 43, 65 and 85). Remove each in its own try and throw one collected failure at the end.
4. **`ResourceDeclaration.provider` is dropped from the attempt tool snapshot** (`ToolConfig.kt`), and the new constructor parameter changes the binary signature of a public plugin-api data class. Document both, or add `@JvmOverloads`.
5. **The timeout test normalizes its own assertion** (`IosSimulatorProviderTest.kt`, around line 270, rewrites `after [0-9.]+m?s` to `after 1s`).
6. **Untested paths:** `xcrun` missing or a spawn failure during `prepare`, a `simctl list` output that does not parse, a `shutdown` failure.
7. **`device_type`, `runtime` and `base` starting with `-`** would be read by `simctl` as an option. Operator trusted input; optionally reject a leading `-`.

Area: `plugins/resource-ios` (`IosSimulatorProvider.kt`, `Xcrun.kt`), `ToolConfig.kt`, `RunCommand.kt`, `AbandonedAttempts.kt`.
