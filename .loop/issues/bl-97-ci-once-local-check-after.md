## What

While `ci: actions` was on, every gate read repeated a build the local merge check had passed minutes before on the same merged head (local 15 to 18 minutes, CI 13 to 15). Counting the local merge check as proof after the first CI run of an issue would have saved 4 runs and 55 minutes in that window. Related: bl-91 (pushing only the merged head saves 59 Actions minutes), and the Actions quota ran out at 00:16 on 2026-11-16 after 373 minutes in that window.

Directions for triage: a `ci` mode that waits for Actions on the first merged head only; or a policy keyed on what changed since the last green run.
