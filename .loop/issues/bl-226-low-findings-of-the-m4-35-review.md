A restart in each cycle resets the counter; each cycle needs a human approval, so slow and visible. 2. **A cancelled, timed out, startup_failure, stale or action_required run counts as red** (`CiWait.failedRuns`) and fires the reaction, spending a `retries` count, a `max_attempts` slot and a turn the agent cannot use. Exclude `cancelled` and `action_required`, or document them; add a test for a cancelled only red verdict.

4. **`reaction_triggered` and the "sends the issue back" comment are written before `onReaction` knows the issue parks** on its last `max_attempts` slot. Same as m4-34; note, or add a line on park.
5. **Findings are in memory only** (bl-224 finding 6); after a restart the retry has `retry_kind` = `reaction` and no failed job names. Persist them, or re-derive at dispatch.
6. **`counted` in `CiFailedReaction` is never cleared**, and job and step names are not stripped of newlines. Trivial.
7. **Test gaps:** a cancelled or timed out conclusion, a restart between a reaction and its approval exit, a reload that adds or removes `reactions:`, a closed pull request.
