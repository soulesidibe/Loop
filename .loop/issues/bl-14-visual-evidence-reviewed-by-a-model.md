## What

Plan 6.4 lists `screenshot` compare and `maestro` flow as plugin check types, and plan 6.9 has the iOS simulator and Android resources. Nothing says yet what a screenshot check compares or how it decides. A pixel diff is the obvious reading of "screenshot compare", and it is brittle: fonts, animations, dynamic data and status bars all differ between runs.

For the pilot app (iOS) and any app later, before and after screenshots attached as evidence would also make the human review of a UI pull request much faster.

Outside reference: Shopify's Helix (shopify.engineering/helix, 2026-09-21) rejected pixel diffing. It screenshots both apps in matching states, and a model acting as design reviewer lists every difference with a severity and a location. It marks a comparison INVALID when the two states do not match, rather than reporting false differences. Fixable differences block by default.

Proposed shape, to settle in triage:

- A `screenshot` check saves before and after captures as evidence, stamped with the workspace fingerprint like other evidence.
- A model reviews the pair and returns a list of differences (severity, location, description) or `INVALID` when the states differ.
- `INVALID` makes the check unable to run (verdict `unverified`), not `refuted`.
- Pixel compare stays possible as an option but is not the default.
- The captures are posted with the evidence summary on delivery (plan 6.5).
