## What

Low findings of the m4-28 review. Triage decides which are worth fixing.

1. **`free` and `takeBack` miss the guard of a lock granted between their two reads** (`ResourceGrants.kt`, `free` around lines 436 to 457, `takeBack` around 307 to 318). `keys` comes from the first `locks.rows()`, the guards are taken, `held` comes from a second read; a lock in `held` but not in `keys` is given back and freed without its guard. Attempt A holds X (prepare booting) and has a queued request for Y; A ends, `free` waits on X's guard; Y is granted to A and the tick starts `prepare(Y, A)`; `free` then releases Y while that prepare runs, frees it to holder C and forgets the key. Results: a leaked device, or two holders' prepares overlapping on one device (Android `waitOrStop` can stop C's emulator; iOS `ofResource(...).forEach(::remove)` removes the other's simulator). Fix: after the read under the guards, if `held` has keys not in `keys`, release and retry with the union until stable; make Android `waitOrStop` stop only its own record (same serial and pid).
2. **The "prepare that ends after its lock was freed is released at once" path** (`prepareInBackground`, around lines 210 to 213) is unreachable through the guarded paths except via finding 1, untested, not holder safe (`provider.release(resource)` takes no holder) and drops the dirty mark. After 1, remove it or check no lock row exists for the resource before releasing, and record the mark.
3. **A tick can still wait on a provider** through the `unfreed` retry (`tick`, around lines 130 to 132, calls `free` inline), up to 10 minutes per call. Run the retry through `inBackground`, or correct the doc sentence "a tick never waits on a provider".
4. **`guards`, `prepareRan` and `endChecked` are not forgotten** for a lock freed by `giveBackAndRelease` (around line 198). Call `forget(listOf(current.key))` on success.
5. **`forget` removes a mutex from `guards` while it is locked**, so a caller arriving right after gets a fresh mutex for the same key. Remove the guard after unlocking.
6. **The plugin-api change is binary breaking too**; an old jar's `prepare` fails with `AbstractMethodError`, caught as a prepare failure. One sentence in `docs/spec-decisions.md` and the `ResourceProvider` KDoc.
7. **A provider that throws `java.util.concurrent.CancellationException`** cancels its background job silently, or at attempt end skips `dropRequests` and `deleteDirectory`. By design; not reachable with the two providers.
8. **An interrupt sent before the pool thread enters `InterruptibleCall.run` is lost**; on `close()` an emulator can boot after the run stopped, left to `recover`. Keep a canceled flag checked at the start of `run`.
9. **`started` written as 0** when `startInstant` is unavailable makes `recover` drop the record and leave the emulator (`AndroidResourceProvider.kt`, `process()`). Fails safe, not reachable on macOS or Linux.
10. **The recorded pid is the `emulator` launcher**; a surviving qemu after a dead launcher is never stopped, and the serial and AVD fallback of `runs()` is dead code after a release. A leak, never a wrong kill.
11. **`inBackground` takes `tryLock` before `launch`**; a job canceled before dispatch never unlocks. Unreachable today (`close()` runs after every guard user). `CoroutineStart.ATOMIC` or unlock in `invokeOnCompletion`.
12. **Old shape iOS simulators** `loop-sim-<id>-<pid>` from an m4-27 build no longer match `NAME` and are never recovered. At most a leftover on this machine, `xcrun simctl delete` by hand, or accept the old shape in `recover`.

Area: `ResourceGrants.kt`, `plugins/resource-android` (`AndroidResourceProvider.kt`, `BootRecords.kt`), `plugins/resource-ios` (`IosSimulatorProvider.kt`), `plugin-api` `ResourceProvider.kt`, `docs/spec-decisions.md`.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** the end of an attempt and a check's take back never give back or free a lock without holding its guard, and no provider call ever releases a resource another holder now has.

**Current behavior:**
- `ResourceGrants.free` and `takeBack` read the attempt's locks, take the guards of those keys, then read again. A lock granted to the attempt between the two reads (its queued request granted by another holder's free) is checked, given back through the provider and freed without its guard, while the tick may be running `prepare` for it in the background. Results: the provider's `release` runs during that `prepare`, the lock is freed to the next holder while the late `prepare` still runs, or two holders' prepares overlap on one device.
- `prepareInBackground` releases a resource whose lock row is gone ("released at once"), with no holder check and dropping the dirty mark, so it can release a resource another holder now has.
- The Android provider's `waitOrStop`, on a failed boot, stops whatever emulator record the AVD has now, which can belong to another holder.
- `giveBackAndRelease` forgets only `givenBack`, so `guards`, `prepareRan` and `endChecked` keep entries for released locks, and a stale `prepareRan` makes a later end release a lock the attempt took again and never prepared.
- `forget` removes a mutex from `guards` while it is still locked, so a caller arriving right after gets a fresh mutex for the same key.
- `InterruptibleCall` loses an interrupt sent before its pool thread started, so a call canceled by `close()` in that instant runs to its end.
- The class doc says "a tick never waits on a provider", but the `unfreed` retry calls `free` inline. The plugin-api change of `prepare(resource, attemptId)` is documented as source breaking only.

**Desired behavior:**
1. `free` and `takeBack` hold the guard of every lock they give back or free. When the read under the guards finds a lock the first read missed, they leave the guards without giving back or freeing anything, take the guards of the union again in the sorted order, and read again, until the read under the guards finds no new key. Only then do they run the checks, give back and free.
2. A prepare that ends after its lock was freed never calls the provider's `release`; the branch is reported as an unexpected state, and the class doc no longer promises the release.
3. The Android provider's failed boot stops only the emulator this prepare started (its own serial and pid).
4. A lock freed after a release leaves no entry in `guards`, `prepareRan`, `endChecked` or `givenBack`.
5. A guard is removed from `guards` only after it is unlocked, so two callers on one key never hold different mutexes at the same time.
6. A provider call canceled before its thread runs it is never run.
7. The class doc states when a tick can wait on a provider (the `unfreed` retry), and `docs/spec-decisions.md` and the `ResourceProvider` KDoc say the `prepare` change is binary breaking too: an old plugin jar fails its first `prepare` with `AbstractMethodError`, caught as a prepare failure.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ResourceGrants`: `free`, `takeBack`, `withGuards`, `prepareInBackground`, `giveBackAndRelease`, `forget`, `guards`, `InterruptibleCall`.
- The Android provider's boot failure path (`waitOrStop`) and its boot records.
- `ResourceProvider` KDoc.
