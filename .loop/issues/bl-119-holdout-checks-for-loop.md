## What happens

The harness supports holdout checks, checks the agent never sees, but the `code` kind in `WORKFLOW.md` declares none. Every check Loop runs on its own work is visible to the agent, which can shape its result to the checks rather than to the issue.

## Proposal

Keep holdout checks for Loop outside the repository, under `$LOOP_HOLDOUT`, for example end to end runs of the CLI against the conformance fixtures, and declare them in the `code` kind.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The `code` kind in `WORKFLOW.md` declares at least one holdout check.
- [ ] The holdout files are not reachable from an agent's workspace.
