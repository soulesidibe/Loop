## What

Low findings of the m4-30 review. Triage decides which are worth fixing.

1. **Control characters reach the operator's terminal.** `TerminalChannel.kt` `oneLine` (around line 38) collapses only `\s+`, and `RunCommand.kt` (around line 437) echoes the line raw to stderr, while `KeyValueLog.kt` (around lines 134 and 142) escapes every control character on the same stream. Agent influenced text reaches it: the first line of a refused merge check (`Merges.kt`, around lines 1192 to 1204), git or gh error text (`PullRequestDelivery.kt`, around line 265), and a runner's failure reason on a `max_attempts` park (`RetryCoordinator.kt`, around lines 466 and 597). An ESC sequence can overwrite the notice with a forged "Next:" line or change the window title. Fix: strip `[\s\p{Cntrl}\u0085  ]+` to a space, the pattern at `Orchestrator.kt` around line 5940, on reason, URL and identifier, ideally in `HumanNotices.tell` so the env is covered too. Tests in `TerminalChannelTest` and the contract. `HumanLog.kt` (around line 238) has the same gap for `loop log`.
2. **The hook runs inline, up to `hooks.timeout_ms` (60 s default) per notice.** A park holds that issue's machine slot and claim and delays the retry queue `save()`; a fresh approval wait holds the attempt's resource locks, slot and workspace; a delivery failure delays the tick's dispatch, reconcile and other waits. Launch the hook (not the channel line or the `human_notified` event) in the run's supervisor scope, or document that a slow hook delays the tick.
3. **`LOOP_NOTICE_REASON` is unbounded in the env.** A park's `summary.reason` has no verified cap; an oversized value makes the spawn throw, reported as the misleading "Loop requires perl on PATH" (`ShellHookRunner.kt`, around line 30), and a NUL makes `ProcessBuilder` throw, so the hook is skipped with `hook_failed`. Cap the env value (about 2,000 characters, the full text stays in the JSON) and strip control characters.
4. **A crash between save and tell loses the notice**, and a kill -9 during the hook leaves the mode 600 `loop-notice-*.json` temp file. Documented as a deliberate choice; `loop status` still shows the wait.
5. **The never twice key is not what prevents duplicates.** `ParkedIssue` holds no key, a park relies on its single call site, and for waits `wait.humanNotice == key` (`HumanNotices.kt`, around line 64) is never true in production. A restored verify wait that parks `unverified` and is killed before its wait ends parks and notifies again on the next start. Store the key on the park record, or correct the PLAN sentence "its key kept in the wait record".
6. **`tell` is not fully guarded** (`HumanNotices.kt`, around lines 88 to 89): `settings()` and the `HumanNotice` constructor (non-blank identifier) sit outside any try. No path produces a blank identifier today. Wrap the body in a catch that logs a warning.
7. **Docs:** warn operators not to interpolate `$LOOP_NOTICE_REASON` into `osascript -e` or `eval`.
8. **A flaky test seen in the m4-30 evidence:** `CommandRunnerTest > stdout and stderr lines are emitted as output` in `plugins:runner-command`, unrelated to the diff. See bl-183.
